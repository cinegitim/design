# Human approval (pointer + gate rule)

Authoritative sources: `AGENTS.md` (model usage policy), `.opencode/agents/brand-director.md` (never declare LOCKED/CANONICAL/APPROVED), `.opencode/skills/brand-studio/SKILL.md` (quality gates).

Standing rule: no direction, logo, or identity may be declared LOCKED, CANONICAL, APPROVED, or PRODUCTION READY on AI authority alone — not by model preference, technical tests, SVG completion, small-size tests, or rendered boards. Final visual approval comes from the human after seeing the actual design. AI review (including senior review) is advisory only. Record human approvals in the brand's `decisions/` log, never infer them.
