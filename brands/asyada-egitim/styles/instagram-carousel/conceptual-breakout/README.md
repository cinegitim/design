# Conceptual Breakout · High-concept carousel

**Status: review-only.** Not a creative sign-off or a replacement for
approved brand identity. Source of truth: [style.json](style.json).

Use when a single meaningful object, typographic tension or controlled
material collage makes a story immediately recognizable. Distinct from
Editorial Campaign (photo/editorial modularity) and Data Atlas
(information grids), this family introduces an expressive **conceptual
device** and a reveal/payoff sequence. Do not repeat one identical poster
on every slide.

Presets: **Object Metaphor**, **Typographic Shock**, **Editorial Collage**.
Axes control the metaphor, materiality, energy, cover, media role and
narrative pacing. Keep the real Jost lettering separately editable
and the *entire exact* canonical H-02 or P-01 SVG lockup unmodified.
An AI image may supply only optional object/photo/background layers.

Use only approved or source-verified supporting images; real HTML/CSS/SVG
must render and be visually reviewed. A visual metaphor must not imply
a false university or scholarship fact. No automatic approval or publishing.
