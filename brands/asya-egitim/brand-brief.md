# Brand Brief — Asya Eğitim (agent-filled)

## Source brief

- Raw brief: premium Turkish education platform guiding students to elite universities in China, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Hong Kong. Must break fully from classic Turkish study-abroad consultancy aesthetics. Concepts: contemporary Asia, academic prestige, discovery, cultural depth, future, selectivity, trust. Strong "Asia" feel without touristic/folkloric/cliché. Audience: high-schoolers, university students, parents — appealing to students, trust-building for parents. Premium, selective, well-connected — not cheap consultancy. China important (future sub-brand) but brand must not read as China-only.
- Brand/topic slug: `asya-egitim`

## Inferred strategy

- Category: premium international-education advisory / placement platform (TR outbound → East/Southeast Asia).
- Audience: 15–24 y.o. students (aspirational, future-facing) + parents 35–55 (risk-averse, prestige- and outcome-driven). Dual register: excitement + reassurance.
- Position / price tier: top-premium; selective, connected, concierge-like. Above classic TR agencies (which skew blue-corporate, photo-of-campus, cap-and-globe clichés).
- Personality: assured, scholarly, contemporary, discreetly ambitious, culturally literate.
- Promise / key message: "Doğu'nun en seçkin üniversitelerine, seçkin bir rehberle." (The bridge is earned, not bought.)
- Competitors noted: classic TR yurtdışı-eğitim agencies (blue/grey corporate, stock campus imagery); global premium education brands (editorial serif + restrained palettes); Asian university brands themselves (crests, vermilion/navy traditions).
- Cultural/market notes: red = luck/prestige in CN but risk-flag in TR finance contexts — use with discipline, never flag-literal. Ink-black + paper + one mineral accent reads scholarly across TR/CN/JP/KR. Avoid national symbols; abstraction over literalism. Bilingual TR/EN naming must work; wordmark "Asya Eğitim" with ğ character needs a typeface with proper Turkish glyph support.
- Real touchpoints (priority): website hero + program pages, Instagram/social proof graphics, brochure + presentation deck for parents, event/fair signage, counseling office interior graphics.
- Constraints: "Asya Eğitim" wordmark with Turkish diacritics (ğ); must not read China-only; must scale from favicon to fair booth.

## Assumptions made

- Name fixed as "Asya Eğitim" (no renaming explored).
- Digital-first but parent-facing print still matters (brochure/deck).
- Tone: TR primary, EN secondary for university names/programs.

## Avoid list

- graduation caps, globes, airplanes, handshakes, flags-as-concept, generic edtech gradients, meaningless geometric AI logos, pagoda/temple clichés (per brief + anti-cliches.md).
