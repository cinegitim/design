# Visual Fidelity Gap — Asya Eğitim (Direction 3 → production)

Source of truth: `explorations/high-aesthetic-direction-3/image-1790766584024.png` (1536×1024, ChatGPT Image, selected direction CURATED PASSAGE).
Production state: `assets/*.svg` (6 files, path-based) + `boards/logo-client-review.html` + PNG + `brand-system.md` (canonical v1).

## 1. What the source had

- **Mark**: ~13–15 hairline slats, much finer than production; aperture is LUMINOSITY (warm-white glow dissolving neighboring slats), with a thin bronze blade alongside — light itself, not a bar.
- **Light**: full chiaroscuro — dark field, glowing aperture, falloff gradients, atmospheric depth.
- **Typography**: high-contrast Didone-like serif, tight elegant caps rhythm ("ASYA EGITIM" + spaced "STUDY IN ASIA").
- **Palette**: near-black ink (#0B0B0C), luminous gold-bronze, glowing ivory — high tension, not muted.
- **Composition**: overlapping panels, full-bleed dark sidebar, cinematic crops, density-vs-openness rhythm.
- **Sub-brand logic**: aperture tint varies per division (bronze / pale / silver) under one construction.
- **Mono**: white slats + glowing aperture on black — luminous, not flat.

## 2. Survived

Concept (selection→focus→passage→possibility); bronze accent role; hierarchy stepping (Asya full / Çin stepped-muted); info-panel triple; sub-brand extension rule; no-cliché discipline.

## 3. Weakened

- Slat fineness (13–15 hairlines → 9 uniform bars).
- Aperture luminosity (glow → flat bronze bar).
- Serif character (sharp Didone → softer, warmer Fraunces — personality shifted editorial-warm).
- Palette tension (luminous gold/black → muted bronze/ink).
- Board tension (cinematic overlap → calm separated cards).

## 4. Disappeared

- Dissolve/fade of slats near the aperture; light falloff and atmospheric depth; tight caps rhythm of the source wordmark; aperture tint variation across divisions.

## 5. Replaced by unnecessary systemization

- "9 slats", slot math, φ-ratio overshoot story (26:16) — invented post-hoc theory with no source evidence. Exactly the over-rationalization the new workflow forbids.
- Fraunces chosen for warmth the source never asked for.

## 6. Production decisions responsible

1. Flat-fill SVG default (glow assumed unreproducible — wrong: linearGradient + feGaussianBlur are vector-native).
2. Early regularization (uniform slats/pitch before studying source variance).
3. Typography evaluated for character in isolation, not against source rhythm.
4. Boards art-directed light-first, abandoning the source's dark-field authority.
5. Geometry documented before appearance was matched.

## 7. Recoverability — FEASIBLE

All losses are recoverable without new concepts: finer variable slats, gradient+glow aperture (SVG-native), Didone-side serif re-evaluation (e.g. Bodoni Moda vs Fraunces, latin-ext verified), dark-field-first presentation, aperture tint system for divisions. No new image generation required to start; reconstruction iterates toward the source.
