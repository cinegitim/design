### Visual inspection of the live logo — four unrelated ideas in one mark

1. **Pagoda silhouettes** — an explicit avoid-list item (national/folkloric
   cliché, and a cheap visual synonym for "China").
2. **Red brush crescent** — the dominant form reads as a *sun/moon crescent*,
   i.e. it codes Japan, not China, on a brand whose entire subject is China.
3. **Wolf silhouette** — no relationship to study-abroad advisory. It also
   carries "wildness/instinct", the opposite of the promised guidance.
4. **Grey mountain range** — a fourth idea, in a fourth colour, competing with
   all three.

Measured consequence: 4 subjects, 4 colours, no single idea; at 120 px the
pagoda rooflines are ~6 px and the wolf's legs are ~2 px. It has no favicon
size, no one-colour variant, and no clearspace rule.

### Verdict

The live identity is a **generic travel-agency aesthetic**: default font stack,
stock national-symbol collage, one raster logo with no lockup system and no
design language. The wordmark, typography, grid, palette discipline, lockup
family and design language all need to be established and then **locked** — the
same discipline applied to Asya'da Eğitim, but an entirely separate identity.

## Inferred strategy

- Category: premium China-focused international-education advisory / placement.
- Audience dual register: 15–24 students (aspirational, future-facing) and
  35–55 parents (risk-averse, outcome-driven). Excitement + reassurance.
- Position: top-premium, selective, locally embedded — the differentiator is
  *both* a 23 000-program knowledge base *and* on-the-ground Shanghai presence.
- Personality: assured, precise, culturally literate, discreetly ambitious.
  **Not** folksy, not touristic, not "adventure travel".
- Promise territory: the student's own record, path and arrival — the
  bureaucracy of an overseas application made legible and navigable.
- Real touchpoints: website (database-heavy, filter UI), programme/university/
  city listing cards, application funnel, social proof, brochure and
  presentation deck for parents, office and fair signage, CRM/mail.

## What the identity must NOT do

Pagodas, temples, lanterns, dragons, clouds, lucky cats, yin-yang, waving
flags, terracotta soldiers, brush-calligraphy wallpaper, red-and-gold festive
clichés, globes, caps, graduation gowns, handshakes, aircraft, generic edtech
gradients, meaningless geometric "AI" marks, stock-photo students.

## What the identity must achieve

- Be unmistakably **scholarly and administrative in authority**, not decorative.
- Survive from 16 px favicon to A0 fair wall with one geometry and one rule set.
- Carry a **lockup family** (stacked / horizontal / compact / small-use /
  formal) with light, dark and monochrome variants, each as a complete locked
  artwork unit.
- Publish a **brand system** (typography, palette, grid, graphic devices,
  pattern, iconography, imagery, material, digital/print/motion) as the single
  source of truth for every later artifact.

## Next: 3 directions

- [ ] Direction A — **KAYIT / THE REGISTER**: the official academic record made
  legible. Concept: bureaucratic record + human navigation. Grammar: strict
  modular grid, hairline rules, tabular numerals, index-card density, one
  vermilion seal mark. (Narrow, severe risk.)
- [ ] Direction B — **KALEM / THE SINGLE STROKE**: ink as economy of gesture.
  Concept: one decisive mark carries you across. Grammar: gesture + void,
  off-grid asymmetry, extreme negative space, one accent. (Brush-cliché risk
  if not constructed on a measurable grid.)
- [ ] Direction C — **KOORDİNAT / THE INTERSECTION**: two coordinate frames, one
  shared axis. Concept: Türkiye's and China's grids meet; the consultancy is the
  intersection. Grammar: graticule, ticks, measured coordinates on every
  element, cartographic wayfinding. (Cold/tech risk for a teenage audience.)

Three concepts, three graphic grammars. Swap test to be applied: palettes
exchanged across all three, concepts must survive.
