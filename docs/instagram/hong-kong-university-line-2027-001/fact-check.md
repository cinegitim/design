# Hong Kong 2027 — fact check (2026-11-08)

**Scope:** 2027 undergraduate entry for international/non-local qualification applicants. Dates are *early, priority or first-round* deadlines, not a universal final application close. The three universities have separate application portals and distinct criteria. No admission or scholarship is guaranteed.

- **CUHK**: advance-offer deadline **12 November 2026**, 23:59 HKT; regular deadline 7 January 2027. Official: https://admission.cuhk.edu.hk/application/overseas-other-qualifications-non-local-international-team/important-dates/
- **HKUST**: priority deadline **25 November 2026**; later rolling applications and overall close 30 June 2027. The official page consulted does not specify a universal hour for the priority date. https://join.hkust.edu.hk/admissions/international-qualifications
- **HKU**: first-round evaluation application deadline **25 November 2026 at 12:00 noon HKT**; later rolling evaluation. https://www.admissions.hku.hk/apply/international-qualifications
- **QS World University Rankings 2027**: HKU #11; CUHK #18; HKUST #33. https://www.topuniversities.com/qs-top-uni-wur
- **CUHK entrance scholarship**: admitted new nonlocal undergraduates considered without separate application; HK$5,000–full tuition, some awards renewable, some may offer allowance up to HK$66,000 per year, subject to selection/terms. https://admission.cuhk.edu.hk/fees-financing-your-studies/scholarships/admission-scholarships-for-undergraduates-only/
- **HKUST academic admissions scholarships**: applicants considered automatically, with awards merit-dependent; some non-academic schemes require separate applications. https://join.hkust.edu.hk/fees-and-scholarships
- **HKU entrance scholarships**: admission application triggers automatic merit-based consideration, including academic and nonacademic achievements. Special awards can have separate applications. https://www.admissions.hku.hk/faqs/international
- Document list is a cross-school preparation guide only; exact evidence, English language test/waiver, interviews and program-specific conditions must be verified on each applicable university/program page. https://admission.cuhk.edu.hk/application/overseas-other-qualifications-non-local-international-team/application-guides/ ; https://join.hkust.edu.hk/admissions/international-qualifications ; https://www.admissions.hku.hk/apply/international-qualifications
