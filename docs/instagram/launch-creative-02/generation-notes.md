# Image-first art direction — Experiment 02

Five distinct gpt-image-2 compositions were generated before any slide HTML or SVG layout was authored. The image model supplied full-bleed photographic collage, atmosphere, crop, paper/ink treatments, movement, and the original typographic/copy apertures. Exact campaign copy and the canonical lockups were added only after art direction, as editable Jost SVG text and verbatim approved lockup SVGs.

| Slide | Image-first visual brief | Reconstruction strategy |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | An expansive contemporary campus opens around an intimate moving student portrait; irregular paper and red acetate sweep across the campus perspective. | Headline in the original torn-paper foreground; support line underneath; small-use Turkish lockup in the ink aperture. |
| 02 | A student enters a high-ceiling passage that turns toward a bright campus horizon; a red vanishing-axis path connects shadow and light. | Large four-line headline in the left paper margin, support on a small irregular paper continuation, stacked lockup on the backpack silhouette. |
| 03 | Five non-uniform urban fragments suggest Shanghai, Tokyo, Seoul, Singapore and Hong Kong through skyline, transit, topography, garden and harbour cues. | Exact thesis and support on a central paper aperture; five varied labels sit on their corresponding photo fragments; a compact lockup stays in the paper field. |
| 04 | Academic study and student friendship/city discovery are interwoven as asymmetric photographs with torn vellum and print overlays. | Headline on the upper-left paper; four program labels orbit separate experiences; one irregular lower annotation carries the exact support sentence. |
| 05 | A dark cinematic city/campus threshold, student silhouette and central cream wave make a strong closing field. | The bilingual lockup becomes the central anchor on the wave; CTA tracks the red sweep; destination line and footer remain editable. |

The generated settings explicitly excluded logos, institutional signage and readable pseudo-copy. The typography treatment in the source images is therefore compositional gesture / negative-space choreography, not final lettering; the production stage sets every Turkish word accurately and keeps it editable. No previous slide image, CSS or component was used as input. The Creative Breakout gallery informed only the ambition and the broad C/A/B ingredients.

Slide 05 received one targeted edit after visual inspection because the first generated frame included an unintended temple-like roof silhouette. The corrected image is the slide-05 source of truth; the initial generation remains preserved and checksummed as provenance. No image-generation tool was asked to create, redraw or typeset any logo.

**Image disclosure:** all people and places are illustrative AI imagery. The five destination images use broad city cues; they are not offered as exact landmark photographs, actual campus photography or evidence about a named institution.
