# Quality review — Rotanı Seç #001

## Stage A — build review

The six first-pass PNGs were inspected together at 360×450 and individually at 1080×1350. The sequence already had strong compositional variety, but supporting copy on slides 03–06 was too small at phone scale. Secondary copy sizes were increased; slide 01 subtitle contrast and slides 02/03 list rhythm were strengthened. No copy was removed or rewritten.

## Stage B — final creative review

- **01 Hook:** asymmetric diagonal collision reads instantly; Tokyo cyan and Seoul acid/pink remain distinct without flags. The title owns the frame; documentary night photography retains atmosphere.
- **02 Life after class:** the contact-sheet rhythm places Waseda, Sinchon, rail and metro in one afternoon narrative. Labels remain readable over the coloured notes.
- **03 Universities:** six real campuses are identifiable without six repetitive cards. Lists are dominant enough to prevent the collage from becoming decorative.
- **04 Language:** English/local-language tension is the sequence's most experimental composition. Japanese `日常` and Korean `일상` both mean “daily life”; they are decorative and accurate.
- **05 Budget:** the four bespoke line icons carry meaning and scan quickly. No unsupported figures or cheaper-city conclusion is introduced.
- **06 Decision:** city choice is visually immediate, comment CTA is dominant, consultation is secondary, and the H-02 signature closes the sequence without a corporate card ending.
