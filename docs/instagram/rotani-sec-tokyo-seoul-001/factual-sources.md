# Factual review sources

The on-slide content is intentionally high-level. No cost figures, ranking claims, programme equivalence or institutional partnership claims were added.

- [Study in Japan — Universities and degree programmes in English](https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/en/planning/learn-about-schools/universities/) — confirms that English-medium bachelor's programmes exist but are programme-specific, and notes daily Japanese helps life in Japan.
- [University of Tokyo — Degree Programs Offered in English](https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/adm/inbound/en/programs-admissions-e.html)
- [Waseda — English-based Undergraduate Programs](https://www.waseda.jp/inst/admission/en/undergraduate/english)
- [Sophia — English-taught Programs](https://adm.sophia.ac.jp/eng/admissions)
- [Seoul National University — Admissions](https://en.snu.ac.kr/admission) — current programme/admissions detail must be checked with the relevant college.
- [Yonsei — Underwood International College](https://uic.yonsei.ac.kr/main/admission.php?mid=m04_01_02) — all instruction in UIC is in English; this does not describe every Yonsei programme.
- [Study in Korea — living and housing](https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/ko/life/livingAndHousing.do) — housing and transport are material student-life considerations.
- [Study in Korea — living expenses](https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/en_US/life/livingExpense.do) — housing, food, transport and other living expenses are distinct budget categories.

The six named universities and neighbourhoods were also matched to the specific licensed photographs listed in `photography-sources.json`. No university logo is used and no partnership or endorsement is implied.
