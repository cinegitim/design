# Research notes — typography round 01

## Market / communication context

- [Pekin Eğitim](https://pekinedu.com/) presents China study pathways as several concrete program types and a guided journey from first consultation through arrival. This informs an emphasis on clear reading hierarchy and practical confidence—not a decorative “China” costume. Its service claims are not adopted as Çin Eğitim claims.
- The brief and locked asset are Turkish-language and symbol-only. The exploration therefore sets only `Çin Eğitim`; it adds no English descriptor, Chinese characters, or unapproved promise.

## Font facts checked

- [Manrope / Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope): open modern sans family; its published family includes variable weights and Turkish Latin glyph support. Used for the clear, single-family digital direction and supporting copy.
- [Source Serif 4 / Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Source+Serif+4): transitional serif designed for contemporary text/display use; published font distribution has Latin Extended/Turkish coverage. Used for an editorial, two-line contrast against the brush symbol.
- [Barlow Condensed / Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Barlow+Condensed): condensed grotesk family with broad weight range and Turkish character coverage. Used for a compact institutional/index-like setting, not as faux-Chinese lettering.

Fonts load from Google Fonts in the HTML proof. System fallbacks are named, but fallback appearance may vary by device. Licensing review is still required before packaging a final production identity; no font files are redistributed here.

## Direction decision

The competitor reference suggests prospective students/families need understandable program and process information. It changes only the type test: B is explicitly tested for immediate, one-line readability; A tests an editorial authority alternative; C tests dense navigation/signature economy. It does not establish audience research or final brand positioning.
